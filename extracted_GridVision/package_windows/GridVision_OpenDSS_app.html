Full methodology (Parts A–L)


    

      
Part A — Purpose and scope

      
GridVision builds a digital model of the BZ0109 low-voltage network and runs a quasi-static time series (QSTS) every 15 minutes for a chosen day.

      
Questions answered: TF net power vs measured LP; phase voltages (measured and simulated); TF loading and voltage risk vs 216.2–243.8 V.

      
Not in scope: transients, faults, protection coordination, live SCADA.

      
Ratings: 250 kVA (options 100/160/250/400/630); nominal ~230 V ph-n; 96 steps/day.

    


    

      
Part B — Input data and preparation

      

        
SourceWhat is used

        
GIS / KMZPoles, coordinates, line segments, connectivity

        
DATA (billing)Account → pole, tariff category, monthly kWh

        
Load profile15-min TF net kW, optional voltages, study days

        
Solar reportAccount, pole, inverter kW

    •

Part C — Network topology

      
    
        
  1. Poles = nodes; lines = edges
    2. 
        
  2. Transformer pole = source (distance 0 m)
    3. 
        
  3. Each first hop from TF starts a feeder (F1, F2, …)
    4. 
        
  4. BFS labels every downstream pole with one feeder
    5. 
        
  5. Distance d = path length from TF; geographic fallback if edge missing
    6. 
      

      
d(TF) = 0
d(k) = sum of edge lengths on path TF → k

    


    

      
Part D — Tariff categories, load models, shapes

      

        
TariffLoad modelTypical daily behaviour

        
R1, R2, D1Constant currentResidential: evening peak

        
GP11, GP12, GP1-GConstant currentMore daytime commercial

        
STL1, STL2Constant currentHigher after dark

        
I12Constant powerIndustrial / flatter or shift-based

      

      
Constant power: P fixed ⇒ I ≈ P/V. Constant current: I fixed ⇒ P ∝ V.

      
P_avg,c = E_month,c / (N_days × 24)
P_c(t) = P_avg,c · s_tariff(c)(t) · α(t)
P_load(t) = P_base + Σ_c P_c(t)

    


    

      
Part E — Solar and net-metering

      
P_pv,i(t) = P_inv,i · c_irr(t) · β
P_pv(t) = Σ_i P_pv,i(t)
P_gross ≈ P_net + P_inv · CF · σ

      
CF (Sri Lanka rooftop) ≈ 14–18% annual. σ = self-consumption (lower ⇒ more noon export). Same account in billing + solar ⇒ net-metered.

    


    

      
Part F — Power-flow calculation and balancing

      
F1. Meaning

      
At time t the network is steady-state: known V_TF, loads, PV, line Z; solve for currents, voltages, TF power. QSTS repeats this 96 times/day.

      
F2. Global balance

      
P_TF,net + P_pv = P_load + P_loss
P_TF,net ≈ P_load − P_pv + P_loss   (import > 0)

      

        
SituationP_TF,net

        
Evening peak, no PVImport > 0

        
High noon PVExport < 0

        
Light load, no PVSmall positive

      

      
F3. One time-step procedure

      
    
        
  1. Fix study time t
    2. 
        
  2. Nodal injections: P_k^inj = P_pv,k − P_load,k
    3. 
        
  3. Slack bus = TF LV (V_TF measured or nominal; angle 0)
    4. 
        
  4. Line model: V_abcn = Z_4×4 · I_abcn (4-wire preferred)
    5. 
        
  5. Load models: constant power or constant current by tariff
    6. 
        
  6. Solve (OpenDSS Newton/fixed-point or radial estimate V_k ≈ V_TF − ΔV)
    7. 
        
  7. Checks: global/local balance, Ia+Ib+Ic+In≈0, |P_TF| vs S_rated, V vs 216.2/243.8
    8. 
      

      
F4. Through the day

      
Night: import, drop with distance. Noon: possible export and remote rise. Evening: max import, often min remote voltage.

      
F5. Loading

      
L% ≈ |P_TF,net| / S_rated × 100

    


    

      
Part G — Calibration and metrics

      
P_sim,raw(t) = P_load(t) − P_pv(t)
P_sim(t) = α(t)·P_sim,raw(t) + r(t)
P_pv*(t) = max(P_load(t) − P_meas(t), 0)   (locked export-target)
corr = corrcoef(P_meas, P_sim)
RMSE_kW = √ mean((P_sim − P_meas)²)
RMSE_V = √ mean((V_sim − V_meas)²)

      

        
PatternLikely issue

        
High corr, high RMSEScale / magnitude

        
Low corrWrong peak or solar timing

        
Good power, bad voltageImpedance, phases, or V_TF

      

    


    

      
Part H — QSTS simulation steps (detailed)

      
QSTS = Quasi-Static Time Series: a sequence of steady-state power-flow snapshots every 15 minutes (96/day), not a dynamic transient study.

      
Step 0 — Prepare once

      

        
ActionPurpose

        
Build network (poles, lines, feeders, distances)Topology for power flow

        
Map customers + tariff models/shapesWho loads where

        
Map PV and net-meter flagsDaytime injection

        
Load LP for study dateP_meas(t), optional V_meas

        
Set TF rating, scales, export-target modeCalibration settings

      

      
Steps 1–8 — Time loop

      
    
        
  1. Pick t ∈ {00:00, 00:15, …, 23:45}
    2. 
        
  2. Build loads: P_c(t) = P_avg,c · s_tariff(c)(t) · α(t); P_load = P_base + Σ P_c
    3. 
        
  3. Build solar: irradiance mode P_pv,i = P_inv,i · c_irr · β, or locked P_pv* = max(P_load − P_meas, 0)
    4. 
        
  4. Balance: P_TF,net ≈ P_load − P_pv + P_loss
    5. 
        
  5. Power flow / radial estimate: solve V_k,φ, currents, losses
    6. 
        
  6. Checks: balance, L%, voltage band 216.2–243.8 V
    7. 
        
  7. Store: P_meas, P_sim, voltages, loading
    8. 
        
  8. Advance t by 15 min until 23:45
    9. 
      

      
Step 9 — After the loop

      

        
Metric / outputUse

        
corr(P_meas, P_sim)Shape agreement

        
RMSE_kW, RMSE_VMagnitude errors

        
Peak snapshotMax import (often ~19:00)

        
Solar-noon snapshotMin net / max export

        
Charts, map, distance plotUI review

      

      
Always: generation + import = load + export + losses. QSTS answers: at each 15-min operating point, what are net power, loading, and voltages?

Part K — Full equations table (with why each is used)

      
Powers in kW; voltages phase-to-neutral in V; t at 15-min steps. BZ0109 · TF 250 kVA · band 216.2–243.8 V · nominal ~230 V.


      
Load and tariff

      

        
NameEquationWhy it is used

        
Average customer demandP_avg,c = E_month,c / (N_days × 24)Converts monthly billed energy (kWh) into a mean kW level so each customer can be placed on the network.

        
Shaped customer loadP_c(t) = P_avg,c · s_tariff(c)(t) · α(t)Applies the tariff daily shape and day/night scale so demand varies over 96 intervals, not as a flat average.

        
Tariff shape s_tariff(t)Normalised profile by class (R, GP, STL, I12, …)Captures when each customer type uses energy (e.g. residential evening peak vs daytime GP).

        
Scale α(t)Day/night (or residual) multiplierMakes the sum of all loads track the measured transformer day without destroying relative diversity between tariffs.

        
Total loadP_load(t) = P_base + Σ_c P_c(t)Fleet demand at time t, including optional unmetered/loss base, for power balance and calibration.

        
Constant-power currentI = (S/V)* with S = P+jQ fixedIndustrial-type loads (e.g. I12): current rises if voltage falls → can worsen under-voltage.

        
Constant-current powerP ≈ |V||I|cosφMost domestic/GP/STL tariffs: power falls with voltage → milder drop feedback.

      


      
Solar and net-metering

      

        
NameEquationWhy it is used

        
PV injection (one inverter)P_pv,i(t) = P_inv,i · c_irr(t) · βModels daytime solar from inverter rating and irradiance-like curve; β adjusts export strength.

        
Fleet PVP_pv(t) = Σ_i P_pv,i(t)Total solar on the feeder at t; needed for net power and midday voltage rise.

        
Irradiance curve c_irr(t)~0 at night, peak near solar noonGives a realistic daytime shape for PV without requiring full weather data every interval.

        
Net-meter gross loadP_gross ≈ P_net + P_inv · CF · σReconstructs load behind a net meter so PV export is not treated as “negative load” only.

        
Capacity factor CF~14–18% annual (Sri Lanka rooftop)Average output ÷ rated power; used when reconstructing gross load from inverter size.

        
Self-consumption σTypically ~0.15–0.50Fraction of PV used on-site; lower σ ⇒ more noon export and higher daytime LV voltages.

        
Export-target solar (locked)P_pv*(t) = max(P_load(t) − P_meas(t), 0)Forces PV so net matches the measured meter when irradiance-only underestimates midday export.

      


      
Power balance and transformer

      

        
NameEquationWhy it is used

        
Global power balanceP_TF,net + P_pv = P_load + P_lossConservation of active power at the LV boundary; defines import/export and the role of losses.

        
TF net (rearranged)P_TF,net ≈ P_load − P_pv + P_lossPractical form to interpret measured vs simulated transformer power (import >0, export <0).

        
Raw simulated TF netP_sim,raw(t) = P_load(t) − P_pv(t)First estimate of import/export before residual/scale calibration.

        
Calibrated TF netP_sim(t) = α(t)·P_sim,raw(t) + r(t)Aligns simulated transformer power to the measured LP curve for that study day.

        
Transformer loadingL% ≈ |P_TF,net| / S_rated × 100Compares throughput to rating (e.g. 250 kVA). Prefer |S_TF|/S_rated if Q is known.

        
Losses P_loss≈ Σ I²R on lines (or folded into residual)Accounts for line heating; often a few percent of throughput in simplified UI paths.

      


      
Power flow (network solve)

      

        
NameEquationWhy it is used

        
Nodal injectionP_k^inj(t) = P_pv,k(t) − P_load,k(t)Local power at pole k for the power-flow solve (PV positive, load withdrawal).

        
4-wire line modelV_abcn = Z_4×4 · I_abcnIncludes neutral and mutual coupling; needed for realistic LV unbalance and neutral current.

        
Branch voltage dropΔV_branch ≈ Z_branch · I_branchLinks current and impedance to voltage change along a segment.

        
KCL at nodesΣ I_branches + I_load − I_pv = 0Current continuity; core of the iterative power-flow solution.

        
Slack / source busV_TF fixed (measured or nominal); angle 0Transformer LV bus supplies whatever P+jQ closes the balance.

        
Radial voltage estimateV_k(t) ≈ V_TF(t) − ΔV(d_k, P_net, P_downstream)UI/planning path when full OpenDSS solve is not run; uses path distance and net flow.

        
Path distanced(TF)=0; d(k)=Σ length on path TF→kPlaces poles on the distance–voltage plot and shows where drop/rise accumulates.

      


      
Metrics and limits

      

        
NameEquationWhy it is used

        
Correlationcorr = corrcoef(P_meas, P_sim)Checks whether simulated TF power follows the shape of the measured day.

        
RMSE powerRMSE_kW = √ mean((P_sim − P_meas)²)Average magnitude error on transformer net power (kW).

        
RMSE voltageRMSE_V = √ mean((V_sim − V_meas)²)Average voltage error where both measured and simulated exist.

        
Voltage band (UI)216.2 ≤ V_ph ≤ 243.8Under/over limits around 230 V nominal for heat map and risk display.

QSTS: evaluate load, PV, balance, and voltage at each t ∈ {00:00 … 23:45}. Calibration (α, r, export-target) makes P_sim track P_meas. Power flow / radial estimate produces V_k for maps and the distance–voltage plot.

    


    

      
Part L — Limitations

      
    
        
  • Shapes are by tariff class, not every customer’s true 15-min meter
    • 
        
  • Export-target matches the meter strongly; use irradiance-only PV for pure what-if studies
    • 
        
  • Simplified radial voltage is not a full unbalanced 4-wire legal audit
    • 
        
  • Q and exact losses may be approximated
    •
