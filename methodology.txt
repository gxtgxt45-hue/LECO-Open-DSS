GRIDVISION — BZ0109 UNIFIED LECO OPEN-DSS METHODOLOGY
=====================================================

1. OVERALL PIPELINE SUMMARY
---------------------------
Step 1: Read GIS, billing, LP, and solar data (accounts normalized).
Step 2: Build network feeders; calculate pole distance from Transformer (TF) along path length.
Step 3: Place customer loads based on tariff category shapes and PV based on net-metering.
Step 4: Scale and apply export-target calibration so TF net power tracks measured Load Profiling (LP) data.
Step 5: Execute Quasi-Static Time Series (QSTS) simulation for 96 intervals (15-min steps).
Step 6: Review net power, voltages, transformer loading, distance plots, and interactive map heat map.


2. LOAD FLOW BEHAVIOR
---------------------
Condition               | What Happens                                   | Voltage Effect
------------------------+------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------
Night / Evening Peak    | Load >> PV -> TF imports (P_net > 0)          | Voltage tends to drop along feeder
Solar Noon              | PV can exceed load -> TF may export (P_net < 0)| Voltage can rise toward feeder ends
Light Load              | Low line current                               | Poles near TF / nominal (~230V)
Phase Unbalance         | Unequal load or PV on Phase A/B/C              | Va, Vb, Vc differ at same pole
QSTS Step               | One steady snapshot every 15 min               | Quasi-static (not transient)


3. MEASURED VS SIMULATED VOLTAGES
---------------------------------
Item                    | Measured (LP)                                  | Simulated (Model)
------------------------+------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------
Where                   | Metered poles / times only                     | Every pole in the model
Phases                  | Often single-phase                             | Phase A, Phase B, Phase C (Va, Vb, Vc)
Evening                 | Voltage falls if recorded                      | Larger drop at long distances
Solar Noon              | Voltage rises if recorded                      | Rise stronger with downstream PV
Limits                  | 216.2 V (under-voltage limit) to 243.8 V (over-voltage limit), nominal 230 V


4. CORE METHODOLOGY (PARTS A - L)
---------------------------------

Part A — Purpose and Scope:
---------------------------
GridVision builds a digital twin model of the BZ0109 low-voltage (LV) network and runs a quasi-static time series (QSTS) every 15 minutes for a selected study day.
Answers:
- TF net power vs measured LP data.
- Phase voltages (measured vs simulated across network poles).
- TF loading % and voltage risk against 216.2V - 243.8V legal limits.
Transformer Ratings: 250 kVA (options 100/160/250/400/630 kVA); nominal ~230 V ph-n; 96 steps/day.

Part B — Input Data and Preparation:
------------------------------------
- GIS / KMZ: Poles, coordinates, line segments, connectivity.
- DATA (Billing): Account -> Pole mapping, tariff categories, monthly kWh.
- Load Profile (LP): 15-min TF net kW, optional pole voltages, study days.
- Solar Report: Account, pole, inverter kW rating.
Preprocessing: Accounts matched after stripping leading zeros; billing cycle codes identify monthly energy; identical account in billing and solar implies a net-metered customer.

Part C — Network Topology:
--------------------------
1. Poles = nodes; lines = edges.
2. Transformer pole = source (distance 0 m).
3. Each first hop from TF starts a feeder (F1, F2, ..., F8).
4. Breadth-First Search (BFS) labels downstream poles with feeder IDs.
5. Path distance d(k) = sum of edge lengths on path TF -> k.

Part D — Tariff Categories, Load Models, and Shapes:
----------------------------------------------------
Tariff Category         | Load Model        | Typical Daily Behavior
------------------------+-------------------+---------------------------------------------
R1, R2, D1              | Constant current  | Residential: evening peak
GP11, GP12, GP1-G       | Constant current  | Daytime commercial
STL1, STL2              | Constant current  | Higher after dark (streetlights)
I12                     | Constant power    | Industrial / flatter or shift-based

Equations:
- Average customer demand: P_avg,c = E_month,c / (N_days * 24)
- Shaped customer load: P_c(t) = P_avg,c * s_tariff(c)(t) * alpha(t)
- Total load: P_load(t) = P_base + sum_c P_c(t)

Part E — Solar and Net-Metering:
--------------------------------
- PV Injection: P_pv,i(t) = P_inv,i * c_irr(t) * beta
- Fleet PV: P_pv(t) = sum_i P_pv,i(t)
- Gross Load Reconstruction: P_gross ≈ P_net + P_inv * CF * sigma
  (CF ≈ 14-18% annual rooftop capacity factor in Sri Lanka; sigma = self-consumption fraction ~0.15-0.50).
- Locked Export Target: P_pv*(t) = max(P_load(t) - P_meas(t), 0)

Part F — Power-Flow Calculation and Balancing:
----------------------------------------------
- Global Power Balance: P_TF,net + P_pv = P_load + P_loss
- TF Net Power: P_TF,net ≈ P_load - P_pv + P_loss (Import > 0, Export < 0)
- One Time-step Procedure:
  1. Fix time t (e.g. 19:00).
  2. Calculate nodal injections: P_k^inj = P_pv,k - P_load,k.
  3. Set Slack bus at TF LV (V_TF measured or nominal ~230V, angle 0).
  4. Solve line equations: V_abcn = Z_4x4 * I_abcn (4-wire execution).
  5. Check nodal current continuity (KCL), loading %, and voltage limits.
- Transformer Loading %: L% ≈ |P_TF,net| / S_rated * 100

Part G — Calibration and Metrics:
---------------------------------
- Raw Simulated Net Power: P_sim,raw(t) = P_load(t) - P_pv(t)
- Calibrated Net Power: P_sim(t) = alpha(t) * P_sim,raw(t) + r(t)
- Correlation: corr = corrcoef(P_meas, P_sim)
- Power RMSE: RMSE_kW = sqrt(mean((P_sim - P_meas)^2))
- Voltage RMSE: RMSE_V = sqrt(mean((V_sim - V_meas)^2))

Part H — QSTS Simulation Steps (Detailed):
------------------------------------------
QSTS runs 96 steady-state power-flow snapshots (every 15 minutes from 00:00 to 23:45).
- Step 0: Build network topology, customer models, solar maps, load LP data, and TF parameters.
- Steps 1-8: Loop through intervals t = 00:00 to 23:45. Compute shaped loads, solar injection, global balance, solve power flow / radial voltage drop, check limits (216.2V - 243.8V), store results.
- Step 9: Post-process outputs (correlation, RMSE, peak snapshot at ~19:00, noon snapshot, voltage profile plots).

Part I — Pole Voltages and Visualizations:
------------------------------------------
- Distance vs. Voltage Plot: X-axis = Distance from TF (meters), Y-axis = Voltage (V). Limits at 216.2 V and 243.8 V.
- Interactive Map Heat Map: Dynamically displays voltage levels across network poles based on time slider position.
- Phase Colors: Phase A (Blue), Phase B (Orange), Phase C (Purple).

Part J — Limitations:
---------------------
- Tariff shapes represent category averages rather than individual customer smart meter curves.
- Simplified radial voltage drop estimation is used for fast UI rendering.
- Reactive power (Q) and detailed neutral currents may be approximated in fast mode.
